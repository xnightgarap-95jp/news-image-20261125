### 1 — GLOBAL — Huawei found guilty of stealing trade secrets

(Nikkei +3.91%, KOSPI +6%, Taiwan Taiex +9%) — that trading day was
24 November, not 25 November. The Real Madrid–PSV match was played on

down on Tuesday in New York and reported worldwide on 25 November.

Senegal boat; the full scope of the CIA/ODNI breach. The Nikkei's
1,600-point intraday fall is the 25 November session, not the
